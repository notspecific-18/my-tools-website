About ToolNest

ToolNest is a small, independent collection of free online tools: calculators, a word counter, an image compressor and a PDF merger. It is built for students, teachers, freelancers and anyone who needs a quick answer without installing software or creating an account.

How it works

Every tool runs inside your web browser. The text you type, the images you compress and the PDFs you merge are processed on your own device and are not sent to a ToolNest server. See the Privacy page for details.

Our approach

Tools should do one job clearly, explain how they work, and show helpful error messages. There is no registration, and we avoid pop-ups. The site is free to use. In the future it may show clearly labelled advertising or affiliate links to help cover costs; if so, they will be marked as such.

Please note

Results are provided for general information. GPA scales differ between schools and universities, so check your institution's official rules. Always keep a copy of your original files before compressing or merging them. The tools are provided as they are, without warranties.
